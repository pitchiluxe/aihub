# AIHub — Product Vision & Architecture (Claude.md)

## Project Name
AIHub

## Mission
Build the ultimate AI intelligence platform that becomes the central destination for AI news, model discovery, tutorials, agent development, prompt engineering, automation workflows, research tracking, and AI education.

AIHub should feel like the Bloomberg Terminal, GitHub, Obsidian, Product Hunt, and Google News for Artificial Intelligence combined into a single modern platform.

---

# Core Vision

AIHub is not another AI chatbot.

AIHub is a real-time AI knowledge hub where users can:

- Discover new AI models
- Track AI company announcements
- Follow AI research breakthroughs
- Learn AI development skills
- Build AI agents
- Create AI workflows
- Learn prompt engineering
- Monitor AI ecosystem trends
- Search AI-only content
- Explore relationships between models, companies, tools, and research

The platform should become the daily homepage for anyone interested in AI.
